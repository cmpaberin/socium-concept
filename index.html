PERMANENT AND CONTRACT
How an engagement runs

Scoped in a week, staffed in three.

  • Week one. Scoping call, then a written team shape with roles, seniority mix, cost and the delivery lead named.
  • Weeks two and three. Pipeline built against the shape. You interview, we handle everything either side of the interview.
  • Week four. Squad starts, with the delivery lead accountable for output rather than attendance.
  • Ongoing. Reporting against your metrics, not ours. Fill rate, time to fill, retention, delivery throughput.
Commercials

Three shapes, depending on who carries the risk.

  • Permanent. Fee on placement, with a replacement guarantee.
  • Contract and TaaS. Monthly per seat, one contract, one invoice, one entity.
  • Statement of Work. Fixed scope, fixed price, we carry delivery risk.
